Render caps

The cap is on our machines, not yours

Local renders are unlimited on every tier. A local render is a drawing call on your own CPU with no phone-home, and counting those would mean adding usage reporting to software running on your machine. We'd rather not, so the only number we count is the one that runs on hardware we pay for.

Previews are free. Scrubbing the timeline returns a single frame, not an encode, so iterating on a chart never draws down the allowance. Only a finished export counts.

At three to eight renders per finished video, 70 a month covers roughly nine to twenty videos. And because the local app keeps working, hitting the cap costs you convenience, not the video — nobody is ever blocked from finishing.

Availability

What ships first, and what's waiting

Rolltape is a local app today: it runs on your machine and renders on your CPU. That half is real and it's what launches. Hosted rendering — the render counts, the priority queue, the extra seats — is waiting on two things, and neither is a scheduling problem we can hurry.

A licensed data feed. The sources the local app reads are fine for personal use and not for redistribution, so hosted rendering can't serve market data to paying customers until that's replaced. Shared infrastructure. The app is built for one process serving one person; hosted needs job state that outlives it.

Both are on the way. When they land, the render allowance appears inside the tier you already pay for.

Commitments

So that "we won't switch things up on you" is structural

Perpetual fallback

Twelve months subscribed earns that version of the local app forever, including after you cancel. Hosted, updates and support stop. The software you paid for does not.

Pause, don't cancel

Creators work in bursts and the quiet month is where subscriptions die. Pause yours instead and pick it up when you're filming again.

Price locked while you stay

The price you sign up at is the price you keep. If it rises for new customers, it doesn't rise for you.

14-day trial, no card

Full Creator features. A trial rather than a tier, so nothing is taken away at the end — nothing was presented as permanent.

Deliberately absent

Three things you won't find here

  • Watermarked output A watermarked chart can't go in the video, which is the entire job. That's a demo that produces nothing usable, not a taste of the product.
  • Render credits They'd meter something that isn't a cost. Our real costs are fixed — the data licence bills the same whether anyone renders or not — and fixed costs want a flat subscription, not a meter that throttles you exactly when you're busiest.
  • A free tier A free tier that later grows a paywall is the failure mode this whole page is designed against, and the cheapest insurance is to never create one. The trial does the same evaluating job without pretending to be permanent.

Prices in USD. Annual billing is ten months' price, on every tier.

The data source is stamped on every chart and can't be removed, on any tier — it's a disclosure about which feed answered, not decoration.
